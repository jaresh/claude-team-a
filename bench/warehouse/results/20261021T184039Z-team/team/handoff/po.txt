Decisions made (for README assumptions):
- po create needs at least one --line (argparse required=True).
- Supplier is free text, stripped, 1 to 100 characters.
- Line quantities go through parse_qty with the product unit; unit cost through parse_unit_cost (4dp). Zero unit cost is accepted.
- Duplicate SKUs on different lines are allowed.
- Over-receipt limit: ordered × 1.05 rounded down to unit precision (each = 0dp, kg = 3dp). Total received including the new qty must be ≤ limit.
- Status: closed if closed; received when every line's received >= ordered; partial if any received > 0; else open. On a closed PO, open is 0 for every line.
- po receive refuses when status is received or closed, when the location is in another warehouse, or when the line is unknown.
- po receive audit target and po close audit target are the PO id (po.create, po.receive, po.close).
- Receipts are tracked in po_receipts and counted only where movements.voided = 0, so voiding a receipt reopens the PO.
- Serial and lot rules are enforced by engine.receive, not re-implemented here.
