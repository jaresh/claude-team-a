Assumptions (for README)
- The po.receive and po.close audit target is the PO id. The spec does not name a target.
- Supplier is stripped and must be 1 to 100 characters.
- A PO needs at least one line. Duplicate SKUs on different lines are allowed.
- Line format is SKU:QTY:UNITCOST, with unit cost up to 4 decimal places (Decimal, no floats).
- A PO is closed by a closed column. Status is derived: closed > received > partial > open.
- A received PO (every line at or over ordered) is status 'received'. Receiving on received or closed is refused. Close is allowed only from open or partial.
- po receive rejects an unknown line number before any qty check. Validation runs before inventory.receive, so a failed receive writes nothing.
