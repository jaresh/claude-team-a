## Assumptions
- reconcile: `--clear` is type=int, so argparse rejects non-numeric ids with exit 2. Clearing a transaction that is already cleared is a no-op, and it still counts as "a --clear given" for the audit entry.
- reconcile: a --clear marks every ACCOUNT posting in that transaction, not just one.
- reconcile: does not call check_open. Clearing marks are not postings, so a closed period does not block them (spec §12 lists posting commands only).
- reconcile: "uncleared" and "cleared_balance" match ACCOUNT exactly, not descendants (as the plan says).
- trial-balance: one row per account name with postings dated on or before --at. Each account's raw balance is converted from its account currency to the book currency with core.convert at the --at date (convert already rounds to cents). Totals are the sums of the rounded debit and credit values.
- ledger: opening and rows include ACCOUNT and its descendants (prefix match on "ACCOUNT:", done with substr, not LIKE). Ordering is date, transaction id, posting id.
- ledger: FROM after TO is a UserError ("bad period: FROM is after TO"). The spec does not cover it.
