## Assumptions
- Validation messages: a malformed principal comes from core.parse_money ("bad amount: X"); a zero or negative principal gives "bad principal: X". Negative rate: "bad rate: X". Months not a positive integer: "bad months: X". Bad start: core.parse_date ("bad date: X").
- A months value whose last payment would fall after year 9999 is rejected with "bad months: X" (otherwise datetime raises).
- Calculation runs in a Decimal context with prec 50. Payment = cents(P·r / (1 − (1+r)^−n)); rate 0 gives cents(P / n). Interest = cents(balance · r). Non-final rows: principal = payment − interest. Final row: principal = remaining balance, payment = remaining + interest, balance 0.00.
- The top-level "payment" is the level payment; the final row's payment can differ by a cent (example: 1000 at 12% for 12 months gives 88.85 each, final 88.84).
- Rate is annual percent: monthly rate = RATE / 1200.
