Assumptions (for the lead to review)
------------------------------------
- Each line is stored as text: quantity as str(Decimal), price via fmt, net and tax via cents. Tax is cents(net * rate / 100), and a line with no tax code has tax 0 and tax_code NULL.
- Tax rate is read from tax_codes.rate at line creation and not stored on the line.
- Numbering is INV-YEAR-NNNN, where NNNN is COUNT of existing numbers with that year prefix plus 1. The year comes from the invoice date, not from the issue date.
- invoice_issue posts Assets:Receivable (+total), Income:Sales (-net), and Liabilities:SalesTax (-tax) only when tax is non-zero. Description is "Invoice NUMBER". Accounts are created with ensure_account.
- invoice_pay needs status issued or partial, a positive amount not above the balance, and an account of type asset in ctx.currency. It posts account (+amount) and Assets:Receivable (-amount), description "Payment NUMBER". Status becomes paid when the balance reaches 0, otherwise partial.
- invoice_show and invoice_get return the same §13 dict. invoice_get is the API entry point.
- invoice_list orders by id and takes an optional status filter.
- report_aging excludes draft invoices. It counts invoices dated on or before --at, and sums only payments dated on or before --at. Buckets are current (not yet due), 1-30, 31-60, 61-90, 90+ (days past due). Amounts are fmt strings; total is included.
- --payables reads bills, bill_lines and bill_payments directly through SQL and does not import bills.py. It has no status filter, since bills have no draft status to exclude.
- customer_add terms must be a non-negative whole number. Default is 30. Errors are "bad terms: X".
- tax_add stores the rate text as given after parse_decimal validation. Duplicate codes are a UserError.
