## Assumptions
- bill_get returns the spec section 14 shape. Line dicts have description, account, tax_code (null when none), net, tax.
  bill_list returns full bill_get dicts ordered by id.
- bill_create: at least one line is required. A repeated (vendor, ref) is UserError "duplicate bill ref: REF".
  Line accounts must exist, be expense or asset, and use the book currency. Tax codes must exist.
  Line nets are not checked for sign (zero or negative is accepted, as parse_money allows).
- Assets:TaxReceivable is created only when the bill's tax total is nonzero. Liabilities:Payable is always ensured.
- bill_pay: status must be open or partial. AMOUNT must be > 0 and ≤ balance. ACCOUNT must be an asset in the book currency.
  Payment date is not checked against the bill date. Only the period-closed check (core.post_transaction) applies.
- bill_pay sets status "paid" at zero balance, else "partial".
- vendor_add terms: int or digit string, zero or more. Negative or non-numeric is UserError "bad terms: X".
- Line pos values in bill_lines are 0-based, as postings are.
- Read commands (vendor list, bill show, bill list) need no user. Writes call require_write.
- bill list --status is validated against open, partial, paid (argparse choices on the CLI, UserError in the function).
- Audit: vendor.add (name), bill.create (bill id), bill.pay (bill id).
