Assumptions (not tested)
- Income is shown positive (-1 times the signed sum of Income accounts). Expenses are shown positive (the signed sum of Expense accounts). net = income - expenses.
- Income and expense reports convert at the `to` date. The balance sheet converts at `at`. Networth converts each month at its month end, in the book currency.
- Zero-total accounts are listed with "0.00" when they had postings in range.
- networth returns [] when from > to. It raises UserError on a malformed month.
- Per-account rounding happens before the totals are summed. In a multi-currency book, total_assets can differ from total_liabilities + total_equity by 1 cent. This is inherent to the per-account convert and is not covered by a test.
- retained_earnings = signed Income sum - signed Expense sum, through `at` (all periods, no closing entries).
