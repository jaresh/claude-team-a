## Assumptions
- collected: invoice lines with status other than draft (issued, partial, paid), dated from..to inclusive. paid: all bill lines dated from..to inclusive (bills have no draft status).
- Period bounds are both inclusive and compared as YYYY-MM-DD text after parse_date.
- from later than to is a UserError ("bad period: X is after Y" (same wording as ledger.py)). The spec does not say this; the alternative is an empty report.
- by_code: a tax code appears when a non-draft invoice line or a bill line in the period uses it, even at zero tax. Lines with no tax code add nothing to totals or by_code.
- Amounts are summed as Decimal from the stored line tax text via core.parse_money, then core.fmt. No conversion.
- Read-only: no user needed, no audit entry.
- The leaf helper _leaf is a private copy of the one in fin/invoices.py (imports of another module's private helper are avoided). The plan does not name a shared leaf helper; core.py has none. Lead may move it to core if wanted.
