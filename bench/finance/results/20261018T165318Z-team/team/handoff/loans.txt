All amounts are fmt strings. It raises UserError for principal <= 0, principal with more than 2 decimals, months < 1, rate < 0.

Assumptions and deviations:
- The command parses PRINCIPAL with money.parse_amount (at most 2 decimals), RATE with money.parse, MONTHS as digits only (int), and --start with dates.parse_date. The plan said "strict decimals" for principal; I added the 2-decimal limit because the schedule is in cents.
- The whole calculation runs inside decimal.localcontext with prec 50. Interest, payments and balances are rounded with money.cents (ROUND_HALF_UP).
- Each non-final month: interest = cents(balance * rate/1200), principal = payment - interest. The final row repays the remaining balance, so its payment = balance + interest.
- RATE 0 uses cents(PRINCIPAL / MONTHS) as the payment; the final row absorbs the rounding difference.
- Row dates use dates.add_months(start, n), so a Jan 31 start gives Feb 28, Mar 31, and so on.
- Read-only: no authorize, no audit, no DB access. It works without a user (--user is not needed).
- --start is required.
