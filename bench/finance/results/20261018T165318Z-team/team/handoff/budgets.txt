Assumptions and deviations
- A negative budget is refused (UserError). Zero is allowed. The spec does not say either way.
- A budget row stores fmt(amount), so amounts have at most 2 decimals (parse_amount rejects more).
- `actual` is rollup(raw_sums(from=first day, to=last day of MONTH))[account][account's currency], so it includes descendants and ignores other currencies. Missing sums give 0.00.
- Re-setting a budget replaces it and is always audited (the set always changes a row).
- `budget set` on an unknown user or a viewer is refused by authorize. `report budget` does not authorize, so a viewer and an unknown --user can read it (same as the other read-only reports).
- Only budgets for MONTH are listed. An account with a budget but no postings shows actual 0.00.
