- Design decisions to keep consistent:
  * Payment account must be an existing asset account (invoice and bill payments; bill payment account is checked as asset too).
  * Bill line account must exist and be expense or asset (checked in _bill_line via get_account).
  * Invoice number = INV-YEAR-NNNN, counter = count of issued invoices in that year + 1 (by invoice date year).
  * Invoices and bills are assumed to be in the book currency (no FX on documents); post_txn enforces the account currency.
  * Invoice price is shown via fmt (two decimals); quantity and tax rate shown as given. Invoices need at least one line.
  * Aging counts only payments dated <= at; invoices included only once issued (number set) and dated <= at.
  * Tax report counts only issued invoices and all bills dated in [from, to]; lines without a tax code are not in by_code.
  * Audit targets are ids as strings (invoice.create/issue/pay, bill.create/pay); party/tax audits use the name/code.
