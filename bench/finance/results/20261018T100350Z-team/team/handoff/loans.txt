Marks go to the `cleared` table with INSERT OR IGNORE (repeats count once). cleared_balance = cleared postings to ACCOUNT dated <= DATE; uncleared = ascending distinct txn ids with a posting to ACCOUNT dated <= DATE that are not cleared.
