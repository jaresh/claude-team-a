Assumptions (for README)
- Conversion: per account, sum unrounded converted values, round to cents, totals are sums of the rounded account values.
- Accounts are filtered by type before conversion, so an unrelated account with a missing rate does not break a report.
- --from after --to is a user error (exit 2). networth checks months the same way.
- --currency is accepted by report income, balance-sheet, networth and trial-balance. aging, tax and ledger have no currency option (aging and tax are book-currency sums of invoice/bill amounts; ledger shows the account's own currency).
- networth converts each month at that month's last day.
- ledger converts each posting to the account's currency at the posting's own date, then rounds per posting.
- Aging: draft invoices/bills are excluded. Payments are summed in Python with Decimal (no SQL SUM on text). Only payments dated on or before --at count. Owed <= 0 is skipped. Bucket by (at - due) days: <=0 current, 1-30, 31-60, 61-90, 90+.
- Tax: lines with tax_code null are skipped. Collected = invoices (not draft) dated in range, paid = bills (not draft) dated in range.

Caveats
- Cross-currency rounding can cause a 1-cent mismatch between total_assets and total_liabilities + total_equity when --currency is used (per-account rounding). In book currency it is exact.
- Balance-sheet and trial-balance use the same per-account rounding, so totals are sums of rounded accounts.
