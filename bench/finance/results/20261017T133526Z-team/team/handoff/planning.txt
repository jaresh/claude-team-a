Assumptions (for README):
- Recurring names are not required to be unique; each recurring is tracked by its id in recurring_done.
- Posting rules for recurring add: at least two postings, every account must exist, at most one omitted amount, and the amounts must balance per currency (a currency with the omitted amount is exempt). Implemented in `_check_postings` in fin/planning.py rather than calling post_transaction, because post_transaction inserts rows and also rejects closed periods (recurring add is allowed for a start in a closed month; the closed check happens at run).
- recurring run: an occurrence in a closed period (or any failing occurrence) raises UserError; the framework rolls back so nothing from that run is created.
- Loan: principal must be > 0 (rounded to cents), months an integer >= 1, rate >= 0 (annual percent). Start date must be a valid YYYY-MM-DD. Principal is rounded with core.q before scheduling.
- Loan rows: payment is the regular payment for rows 1..N-1; the last row's payment is its remaining balance plus its interest (may differ by a cent or so from the regular payment). total_interest is the sum of the per-row rounded interests.
- The "payment" top-level field is the regular payment, not the last row's.
