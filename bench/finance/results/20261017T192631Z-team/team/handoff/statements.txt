Assumptions for README.md (lead to add):
- Report amounts are converted per currency at the report date (core.convert, unrounded), rounded once per account; totals are rounded once from unrounded sums. In multi-currency books a total can differ by a cent from the sum of its rounded rows.
- Income report: income shown as negated raw balance; expenses as raw balance; net = total_income - total_expenses. Date range is inclusive on both ends.
- Balance sheet: retained_earnings = -(sum of income and expense raw balances) up to --at; total_equity = total_assets - total_liabilities (so the identity holds exactly).
- Net worth: one row per month from --from to --to, evaluated as of the month's last day; networth = assets - liabilities.
- Trial balance: book currency; each account with a non-zero rounded balance is a debit or credit row; accounts with zero balance appear with "0.00" in both columns only if they have postings (as sum_postings returns them).
- Ledger: the account and its descendants (subtree). Each posting is converted to the ACCOUNT's currency at its own transaction date and rounded per posting, so rows add up exactly. Postings before --from fold into "opening". Rows sorted by date, id, posting id.
- Reconcile: --clear ids must each have a posting to the account dated on or before --date, otherwise UserError and nothing is marked (whole command atomic via execute's rollback). Cleared marks persist across runs; uncleared = ascending distinct txn ids dated on or before --date that are not cleared. difference = statement balance - cleared balance. An audit entry "reconcile" (target = account) is written only when --clear is given.
- Reconcile requires editor/admin (write level); viewers can run reports.
