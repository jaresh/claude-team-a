- Design, from spec sections 8 and 17:
  * Amounts are grouped by (account, currency), summed, then converted once with book.convert at the report date (--to for income and ledger, --at for balance-sheet and trial-balance, month end for networth). The same helper, `_sums`, is used for every report.
  * Income and liabilities/equity are shown as negated raw balances. Accounts appear only if they have postings in range, so zero-sum accounts show "0.00".
  * retained_earnings = -(sum of income and expense raw balances) up to the date.
  * ledger converts each posting to the account's currency at --to. Opening is the sum of the converted postings before --from, over the account and its descendants.
