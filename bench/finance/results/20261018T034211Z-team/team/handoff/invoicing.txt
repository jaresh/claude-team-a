Assumptions for README:
- Invoice and bill lines: DESCRIPTION;A;B;TAXCODE (4 fields, ';' separated, so descriptions cannot contain ';'). Empty TAXCODE means no tax.
- Invoice line: A = quantity (> 0), B = unit price (>= 0); net = q2(qty*price); tax = q2(net*rate/100) per line; invoice totals sum the rounded line values.
- Bill line: A = amount (>= 0), B = expense or asset account (must exist).
- Tax rate must be >= 0; customer/vendor --terms must be >= 0 (default 30 days); due = date + terms.
- Invoice numbers: INV-YYYY-NNNN by the invoice date's year, sequential per year, assigned at issue. Ids (for show/issue/pay) are per kind, 1-based.
- Invoice create requires at least one --line and is allowed in a closed period; issue posts on the invoice date and is refused in a closed period.
- Issue postings: Assets:Receivable +total, Income:Sales -net, Liabilities:SalesTax -tax (only when tax != 0). Accounts are created as needed.
- Payments (invoice pay / bill pay): amount rounded to cents, must be > 0 and <= balance; the account must be an asset account; date must be open (closed period refused). Invoice pay posts Account +amt / Assets:Receivable -amt; bill pay posts Liabilities:Payable +amt / Account -amt. Payment dates are not required to fall after the invoice date.
- Draft invoices cannot be paid. Status: invoice draft -> issued -> partial -> paid; bill open -> partial -> paid.
- Bill create: --ref must be unique per vendor; bill date must be in an open period; postings per line are account +net, Assets:TaxReceivable +tax, Liabilities:Payable -total.
- Aging: buckets by days past due (current = not yet due, 1-30, 31-60, 61-90, 90+); drafts excluded; balances as of --at count only payments on or before --at; zero balances omitted.
- Tax report: range is inclusive on invoice/bill date; drafts excluded; collected = invoice line tax, paid = bill line tax; net = collected - paid; by_code lists every code used in range (even with zero tax).
- Names (customer, vendor, tax code, ref) are stripped of surrounding spaces.
