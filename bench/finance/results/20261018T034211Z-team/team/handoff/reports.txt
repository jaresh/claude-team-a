Assumptions (for README)
- Each account is converted at the report date and rounded to cents per account; totals are sums of the rounded values.
  In multi-currency books a total can differ from the sum of unrounded values by a cent. Single-currency books
  balance exactly.
- income and balance-sheet take --currency (default book currency). networth takes --currency too.
  trial-balance has no --currency and always uses the book currency.
- income: --from and --to are inclusive dates; --from after --to is a user error. Accounts with postings in the
  range are listed even when their total is 0.
- balance-sheet: retained earnings = income total minus expense total up to --at; total_equity = equity + retained.
- networth: one row per month from --from to --to (YYYY-MM, inclusive), balance as of that month's last day.
- ledger ACCOUNT: includes postings to descendant accounts (ACCOUNT:...). Rows are ordered by date then id.
  opening = balance before --from; closing = balance after the last row through --to. Each posting in another
  currency is converted at its own date; amounts are in the account's currency.
- loan schedule: principal and rate are validated (principal > 0, rate >= 0, months >= 1). Payment is rounded
  to cents. The last row pays the remaining balance plus its interest. Dates use add_months from --start, with
  month-end clamping. Rate 0 gives an equal split of principal. Nothing is stored.
- reconcile: every --clear transaction must have a posting to ACCOUNT dated on or before --date; otherwise the
  whole command fails with no changes. Clearing sets postings.cleared = 1 for that account only. An audit entry
  ("reconcile", ACCOUNT) is written only when at least one --clear is given. cleared_balance sums cleared postings
  on the exact account up to --date. uncleared lists transaction ids with an uncleared posting on that exact
  account up to --date, ascending.
- reconcile requires --user with write permission (viewer refused). Reports need no user.
