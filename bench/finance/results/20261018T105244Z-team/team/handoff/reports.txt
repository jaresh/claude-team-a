- Assumptions, to list in README.md:
  - Income/liability/equity show as negated raw balances; expenses and assets as raw.
  - Conversion: each account's per-currency sum is converted at the report date and rounded, then summed (per spec 8).
  - networth = assets - liabilities (liabilities positive), per month at month-end.
  - ledger converts every row at the --to date; opening is converted at --to too, so the running balance is in ACCOUNT's currency.
  - retained_earnings = income - expenses as shown; balance-sheet and trial-balance totals are sums of per-account rounded values.
- Known caveat: with several currencies, rounding each account separately can leave a cent-level gap in the balance-sheet identity (total_assets vs total_liabilities + total_equity) or the trial balance (total_debit vs total_credit). The single-currency case is exact. The tests check equality only for the mixed-currency data they use, where it holds. If the lead wants exact equality in all cases, a decision is needed on where the rounding residual goes.
