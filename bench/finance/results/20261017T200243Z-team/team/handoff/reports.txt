Assumptions (also belong in README):
1. Rounding: each account's figure is converted (each currency subtotal at the report date, unrounded), then rounded to cents per account. Totals are sums of rounded account figures. Retained earnings = total income − total expenses from the same rounded figures. In single-currency books the identities hold exactly. In multi-currency books, per-account rounding can leave a cent or so of difference, so total_assets may differ from total_liabilities + total_equity, and trial-balance total_debit may differ from total_credit. I did not add a plug. Tests only check the identities on books where conversions are exact.
2. A currency with postings in scope needs a rate even if its sum is zero (missing rate → exit 2). Inverse rates work (via core.rate).
3. Income, ledger and networth: --from after --to is a user error. Report months/dates are validated.
4. `--currency` is accepted on income, balance-sheet, networth, trial-balance. Not on ledger (it reports in the account's currency).
5. ledger: each posting is converted to ACCOUNT's currency at its own date and rounded per row; opening and running balance are sums of those rounded row amounts. Descendants matched with substr (not LIKE). Also gets a posting's real currency if it differs from the account's.
6. networth: networth = total_assets − total_liabilities per month end (equity not separately added).
7. reconcile: perm is 'write' (so viewers can't run it, even read-only without --clear). Audit `reconcile` (target = account) only when at least one --clear is given. --clear values must be integers and each must have a posting to ACCOUNT dated ≤ DATE; validation runs before any mark is written. cleared_balance counts ACCOUNT's own postings in cleared transactions dated ≤ DATE (not descendants).
