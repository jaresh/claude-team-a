Assumptions for README.md (I do not own README; the lead or httpapi should add these):
- Import: CSV columns default to 0 date, 1 description, 2 amount; --skip-header drops the first non-blank record; blank lines are ignored; description is stripped; the amount is rounded half-up to cents.
- Import: the category is the first rule (case-insensitive regex search) that matches the description; otherwise Income:Uncategorized (amount > 0) or Expenses:Uncategorized. Rule accounts and Uncategorized accounts are created on demand in the book currency.
- Import: a row already in the imported table (same account, date, description, amount) is skipped, including repeats within one file. The whole import is all-or-nothing; a bad row or a closed-period row raises a UserError that rolls back everything. The "line" in errors is the CSV record number.
- Rule add: pattern must be non-empty and compile as a regex; the account must be a valid account name (it is not created at rule add).
- Budgets: only on existing expense accounts; amount is rounded to cents and must not be negative; set replaces an existing budget for the same account and month. Budgets are in the account's currency.
- Report budget: actual = postings under the account (including descendants) dated in the month; postings in another currency are converted at the month end. Remaining = budget - actual. Accounts are ordered by name.
- Recurring: names are unique and are checked with books.check_name. Postings may have one blank amount, which takes the balancing amount. Monthly and yearly occurrences keep the start day, clamped to month end (2028-01-31 gives 2028-02-29). Weekly adds 7 days per occurrence.
- Recurring run: processes all items by name up to --until (inclusive). Each (item, occurrence) is created at most once. A closed-period occurrence raises a UserError and rolls back the whole run. Audit "recurring.run" target is the until date, written only when something is created.
- Audit: import is written only when rows are added (target: account); budget.set target "ACCOUNT MONTH"; rule.add target: pattern; recurring.add target: name.
