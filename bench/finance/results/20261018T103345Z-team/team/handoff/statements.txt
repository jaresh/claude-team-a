Assumptions (for README)
- Reports convert each (account, currency) sum at the report date (--to for income, --at for balance-sheet and trial-balance, each month's last day for networth) with core.convert, sum unrounded, then fmt once.
- Income report: accounts are listed by the name their postings are on, only if they have postings in [--from, --to]. Income is the negated raw sum, expenses the raw sum. net = total_income - total_expenses.
- Balance-sheet: assets as raw sums; liabilities and equity negated; retained_earnings = -(raw income + raw expenses) up to --at; total_equity = equity + retained_earnings.
- Networth: one row per month from --from to --to (inclusive), as of month end. networth = assets - liabilities. --from after --to is a user error (same for income and ledger).
- --currency is accepted by income, balance-sheet and networth (section 8). trial-balance always uses the book currency (section 17 says so); ledger always uses the account's currency.
- Trial-balance: rows are accounts with postings on or before --at (by posting name, sorted). Debit = positive balance, credit = negative balance as a positive number, other side "0.00". total_debit and total_credit are sums of unrounded balances, so they are equal in the book currency (individual rows are rounded and may differ from totals by a cent in multi-currency books).
- Ledger: postings to the account and its descendants up to --to. Each posting is converted into the account's currency at its own date (identity when currencies match). opening = running balance before --from; rows are in-period postings with amount and running balance (raw signed sums, no sign flip); closing = last running balance.
- --from and --to are required for income, networth and ledger; --at for balance-sheet and trial-balance.
