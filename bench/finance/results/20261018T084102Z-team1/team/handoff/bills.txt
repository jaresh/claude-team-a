Assumptions
- Bill line amount (net) must be > 0 with at most two decimals; a line description is required.
- Bill ref is required (trimmed, non-empty) and unique per vendor.
- Bill needs at least one line; line tax code optional (empty = no tax); unknown code is a UserError via invoices.tax_rate.
- Payment amount must be > 0, at most two decimals, and no more than the balance; the payment account must be an asset (mirrors invoice pay).
- Payment dates are checked against closed periods via book.post; bill create checks them the same way.
- Bill status: open -> partial -> paid. A paid bill cannot be paid again.
- report tax: from > to is a UserError; by_code includes every non-null tax code used by a line dated in the period, even with zero tax; codes with no lines in the period are omitted.
- Draft invoices are excluded from collected tax (matches invoices aging); bills have no draft state.
- Vendor terms: non-negative integer days, default 30; due = bill date + terms.
- Line accounts must exist and be expense or asset. Bill posting is per currency (book.post), so a line account in another currency than the book makes the transaction fail as unbalanced; not handled specially.
