ASSUMPTIONS
- Quantity must be > 0; price must be >= 0; a line needs a non-empty description and exactly four
  ';'-separated fields (the tax code may be empty).
- Tax rates are stored and returned as the given string (e.g. "20"); negative rates are rejected.
- Tax amount = q2(net * rate / 100), ROUND_HALF_UP per line; totals are sums of line values.
- Customer terms are a non-negative integer in days, default 30; due = invoice date + terms.
- Invoice numbers are INV-YYYY-NNNN, counted per invoice-date year over issued (non-draft) invoices.
  Issue order is what matters; drafts never take a number.
- An invoice needs at least one line. A zero-total invoice can be issued.
- Issue posts Assets:Receivable total, Income:Sales -net, and Liabilities:SalesTax -tax when tax != 0.
  Receivable, Sales and SalesTax accounts are created in the book currency if missing.
- Payment amount must be > 0 and have at most two decimals; it cannot exceed the current balance.
  The payment account must be an existing asset account (unknown account is a user error).
- Payment date is not checked against the invoice date; it must be a valid date and not in a closed period.
- Payment posts account +amount and Assets:Receivable -amount; status becomes partial or paid.
- Invoice ids must be digits; an unknown id is a user error.
- `invoice list --status` accepts draft, issued, partial, paid; anything else is a user error.
- Aging counts issued invoices dated on or before `at`, with only payments dated on or before `at`.
  Balance <= 0 is skipped. Days overdue = at - due; bucket "current" when <= 0.
- Aging with --payables is delegated to fin.bills.aging(book, date).
