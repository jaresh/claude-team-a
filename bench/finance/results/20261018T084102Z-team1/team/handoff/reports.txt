Assumptions (for README.md, lead to merge):
- Income and expenses are converted per (account, currency) at the period end date (--to).
- Balance sheet and trial balance convert at --at; networth converts at each month's last day.
- Ledger converts to the account's own currency at --to (rows and opening alike); balances are in the account currency.
- Networth = total assets - total liabilities (equity not included).
- Income, liability and equity are shown as negated raw balances; assets and expenses as raw.
- Retained earnings = income - expenses to date (all time, up to --at), added into total_equity.
- Trial balance is always in book currency (no --currency, per spec section 17); debit/credit rows are the sign of each account's balance; zero balances show as 0.00 / 0.00.
- --from after --to is a UserError (income, networth, ledger). Invalid dates/months/currencies are UserErrors.
- Ledger includes descendant accounts (e.g. "Assets" includes "Assets:Bank").
- Reports need no --user (read-only), and viewers can run them.
