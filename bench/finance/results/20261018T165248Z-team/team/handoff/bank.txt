**Assumptions (for README, lead to merge)**
- Import: fully empty CSV lines are skipped; a row with fewer columns than needed is an error naming the line; negative column index is an error.
- Import: uncategorized account is created via core.ensure_account in book currency, so importing into an account in another currency fails as an unbalanced transaction.
- Import: dedup key stores amount via core.fmt; repeats within one file count as skipped.
- Rule patterns are case-insensitive regexes (re.IGNORECASE); first matching rule by add order wins.
- Budget: only expense-type accounts may have budgets; negative amounts are rejected; set replaces the month's value.
- Budget actual: includes descendant accounts; per-currency totals are converted to book currency at month end with core.convert, then summed; remaining = budget - actual (may be negative).
- Recurring: name must be unique and not empty; start and stored postings are validated at add time (balanced, at least two postings, known accounts); run creates every occurrence up to --until not yet created (tracked per name+date); a closed period in that range makes run fail with a UserError and nothing is created.
- Reconcile: --clear ids must be integers with a posting to the account on or before --date (otherwise UserError); cleared marks persist per account; cleared_balance sums cleared postings up to the date; uncleared is the sorted list of txn ids with postings to the account that are not cleared.
