- Assumptions made where the spec was silent (flag these if the lead wants different behaviour):
  * Unit price is stored with core.money (rounded to cents). Line net = r2(quantity * price). Tax = r2(net * rate / 100) per line.
  * Quantity must be > 0. Price must be >= 0. Bill line amount (net) must be >= 0. At least one line is required on invoices and bills.
  * Bill line accounts must be expense or asset accounts. Payment accounts for both invoices and bills must be asset accounts.
  * Bill ref is unique per vendor.
  * Draft invoices are excluded from aging and from the tax report. Issued invoices get INV-YYYY-NNNN numbers by counting existing numbers with that year prefix, so the number is assigned at issue time, not create time.
  * Tax report by_code includes a code if any line in the period uses it, even when that code's tax is zero for the period (the acceptance test needs VAT5 with paid 0.00).
  * Tax-report amounts use `Decimal(r["tax"])` from the stored TEXT values.
- Posting accounts used on issue: Assets:Receivable, Income:Sales, Liabilities:SalesTax. On bill create: the line accounts, Assets:TaxReceivable (only if tax is non-zero), Liabilities:Payable. The acceptance test expects these names.
- Errors are raised as UserError with no DB change. This relies on Ctx.run wrapping write handlers in a transaction, as the lead's cli.py does.
