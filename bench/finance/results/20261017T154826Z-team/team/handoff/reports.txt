Assumptions (for README)
- budget report: actual = postings to the account and its descendants in the month, converted to the book currency at month end. No --currency flag.
- income / balance-sheet / trial-balance: --currency converts each posting at the report date (--to or --at). Missing rate -> UserError (exit 2).
- income: from > to is a user error. networth: from > to is a user error. Months must be YYYY-MM.
- balance-sheet: totals are built from per-account rounded values. total_equity = total_assets - total_liabilities (so the identity holds to the cent). retained_earnings = total_equity - sum of equity accounts (the balancing figure; equals income minus expenses when no conversion is involved).
- networth: assets and liabilities in book currency, converted at each month's last day. networth = assets - liabilities. No --currency flag.
- trial-balance: rows for every account name with postings up to --at. If per-account rounding leaves a residue, it goes onto the largest balance so debit and credit totals are equal.
- ledger: in the account's currency. Each posting is converted at its own date (no-op when the currency matches). Row id = transaction id. Opening = balance before --from, including descendants.
- Rows and accounts are listed by the posting's account name (not rolled up), except budget and ledger which use prefix matching.
