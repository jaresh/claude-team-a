Assumptions (for README)
- Invoice numbers INV-YYYY-NNNN, assigned at issue, counted per year (no delete command exists, so no gaps).
- Bill and invoice ids are per kind, assigned at create (bills) and create (invoices, draft ids).
- Due date = date + party terms (customers default 30).
- Invoice issue posts Assets:Receivable (total), Income:Sales (-net), Liabilities:SalesTax (-tax, if any).
- Bill create posts each line's account (net), Assets:TaxReceivable (tax), Liabilities:Payable (-total). Line accounts must be expense or asset in book currency.
- Payments: invoice pay posts to the given asset account (+) and Assets:Receivable (-); bill pay posts Liabilities:Payable (+) and the given asset account (-). Payment cannot exceed balance; drafts cannot be paid; Assets:Receivable cannot be a pay account.
- Tax is rounded per line (core.q). Lines with no tax code carry zero tax and are excluded from the tax report.
- Status: invoice draft/issued/partial/paid; bill open/partial/paid. Bill ref must be unique per vendor.
- Aging: buckets use days past due as of --at, counting only payments dated on or before --at; drafts excluded; "current" = not yet due.
- Tax report: only non-draft documents dated in range; collected from invoices, paid from bills.
- Reconcile: --clear marks transactions with a posting to that account on or before --date (stored in cleared table, persists across runs); reports cleared balance, difference, and uncleared transaction ids.
