- Rules I chose where the spec is silent (all in the code):
  - An invoice needs at least one line (UserError).
  - Quantity stored and shown as given; price shown with fmt; line net = q2(qty*price), tax = q2(net*rate/100).
  - Issue: number counts invoices with a number and an invoice date in the same year, +1. Closed-period check runs before numbering; add_txn also checks.
  - Pay: amount must be > 0 with at most two decimals (no silent rounding); payment account must exist and be type asset; overpay, draft or paid invoice -> UserError.
  - Aging/tax report treat any non-draft invoice as issued (there is no issue timestamp, so a later-issued invoice dated before DATE is counted by its current status). Aging balance = total minus payments dated <= DATE; invoices with balance <= 0 are left out. Buckets: days past due <= 0 current; 1-30; 31-60; 61-90; 90+ is anything above 90.
