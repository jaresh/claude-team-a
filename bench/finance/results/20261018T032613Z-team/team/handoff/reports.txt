Design notes
- Shared helper _sums(book, target, at, start, end) groups postings by (account, currency), sums in Decimal, then converts once per group with book.convert at the report date. Rounding happens once per output account (fmt/q2).
- Totals are sums of the rounded per-account values, so displayed figures add up.
- retained_earnings = sum of rounded income minus sum of rounded expenses (up to --at).
- networth: assets and liabilities are rounded only at the end of each month; networth = fmt(assets) - fmt(liabilities).
- ledger: each posting is converted to ACCOUNT's currency at its own posting date. Rows carry the transaction id (t.id). Opening = postings dated before FROM. Closing = last running balance.
- trial-balance has no --currency option (spec says book currency only).
- Choices not in the spec: --from after --to is a UserError for income, networth. Empty ranges give empty results. Income/balance-sheet convert every posting in range, so a USD posting with no rate is a UserError even if it is not income-related (consistent with "missing rate is a user error").

Caveats
- With FX, per-account rounding can leave a cent of drift, so total_assets may differ from total_liabilities + total_equity by 0.01 and trial-balance debit/credit totals may differ by 0.01. Exact for single-currency books (all tests use EUR only, plus one USD case that is exact at 0.9). If the lead wants an exact guarantee, tell me and I will change the rounding approach.
