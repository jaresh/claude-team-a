Assumptions (beyond the spec)
- Line AMOUNT is rounded to cents (half up) and must be >= 0; a negative amount is a user error.
- Line and payment accounts must be in the book currency, else a user error. Without this, post_txn fails with a confusing "does not balance".
- Payment amount must be positive and have at most two decimals; more than the balance is an error.
- Payment date is not checked against the bill date. Closed periods are caught by post_txn.
- Bill and payment status: open (no payments), partial, paid.
- Payable and TaxReceivable accounts are created only when needed (TaxReceivable only if the bill's tax is non-zero).
- A zero-tax line still records its tax_code in output.
