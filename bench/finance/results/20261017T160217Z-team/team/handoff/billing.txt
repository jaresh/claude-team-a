Assumptions for README (billing)
- Foreign currency: billing works in the book currency only. Accounts used by billing must be in the book currency, else UserError.
- Invoice unit price is stored and shown exactly as entered (not rounded). Line net = round(quantity x price) to cents; line tax = round(net x rate / 100).
- Line format is strictly DESCRIPTION;QUANTITY;PRICE;TAXCODE. The TAXCODE field may be empty but its semicolon is required ("A;1;10;" not "A;1;10").
- Invoice numbers INV-YYYY-NNNN count issued invoices per year of the invoice date; drafts get no number. Invoice and bill ids start at 1 each.
- Bill ref must be unique per vendor (not globally).
- Payments must be positive. Bill pay accepts an asset or liability account (spec does not restrict it); invoice pay requires an asset account.
- report tax does not reject from > to (it returns zeros/empty for an empty range).
- Aging and tax reports ignore drafts. Invoices with zero balance are omitted from aging.
