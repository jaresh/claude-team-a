Design points for the lead and the consistency pass
- command_group(sub, "report") in fin/banking.py: reuses the existing "report" group or creates it (dest="subcmd", required, parser_class=cli.Parser). Reports and billing must use it. Calling sub.add_parser("report") twice fails. I messaged main, reports and billing about this.
- Import: categories are created with the bank account's currency, not the book currency, so postings balance for non-book-currency accounts. Dedupe key: (account, date, description, amount as fmt string). Categories: Expenses:Uncategorized for negative amounts, Income:Uncategorized for positive ones; rule matches go to the rule's account.
- Recurring run: occurrences are dated by core.add_months(start, k, day=start.day) for monthly and yearly, and start + 7k days for weekly. Each (recurring_id, date) is recorded in banking_recurring_done so reruns create nothing new. A closed period raises and rolls back the whole run.
- Reconcile: cleared_balance is the sum of account postings in txns dated on or before --date, with cleared IDs recorded in banking_cleared (INSERT OR IGNORE). Each --clear ID must be a posting to ACC on or before --date, or the command fails and nothing is marked.
- report budget: actual is the sum of direct postings per month in each currency, added as-is. Remaining = budget - actual.
