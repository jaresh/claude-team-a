Assumptions (for README, which I do not own)
- Bill line AMOUNT >= 0 (zero allowed); line format DESCRIPTION;AMOUNT;ACCOUNT;TAXCODE, empty TAXCODE means no tax.
- Line account must be an expense or asset account; accounts must exist.
- Tax per line = round half up(net * rate / 100) to cents; total = sum of line net + sum of line tax.
- Bill ref is unique per vendor (UNIQUE(vendor, ref)), blank ref refused.
- Bill due = bill date + vendor terms (default 30 days).
- Bill create posts: each line account +net, Assets:TaxReceivable +tax (only if tax != 0), Liabilities:Payable -total. Description "Bill REF".
- Bill pay account must be an asset; amount > 0 and <= balance; posts Payable +amount and account -amount, description "Payment REF". Status becomes paid when balance hits zero, else partial.
- Paying a bill that is paid is refused. Closed periods block create and pay (via core.post_txn).
- Payables aging: bills dated on or before --at; balance = total minus payments dated on or before --at; bucket by days past due (due to --at), same buckets as receivables; zero or negative balances are left out.
- report tax --from/--to inclusive: collected = tax on issued (numbered) invoice lines with a tax code, dated in range (drafts excluded); paid = tax on bill lines dated in range. by_code lists every code used, including zero-rate ones. net = collected - paid. --from after --to is refused.
- Audit: vendor.add (name), bill.create (bill id), bill.pay (bill id).
