Assumptions to list in README.md
- Rounding: every amount goes through core.q (ROUND_HALF_UP to cents) and fmt; no float anywhere.
- Currency: with --currency, or for postings in a foreign-currency account, each account's sum is
  converted once at the report end date via core.convert, which rounds per account. For this reason
  the balance-sheet identity (assets = liabilities + equity) and trial-balance debit = credit can
  differ by a cent on books with foreign-currency accounts. Single-currency books are exact.
- Zero amounts in a foreign currency need no rate. Missing rate raises UserError.
- Report accounts appear only if postings sit on that exact account name. Parent totals appear only
  where a parent account itself has postings.
- Balance sheet: retained earnings = income - expenses through the report date; the sign is
  applied so that assets are positive and liabilities/equity are shown as positive credits.
- Networth: month-end balances; --from/--to are YYYY-MM; from must not be after to.
- Ledger: includes descendant accounts; postings in other currencies are converted into the
  account's currency at each transaction's date; opening is the balance before --from.
- Loan schedule: principal must be positive, rate must not be negative (0 gives equal payments),
  months must be at least 1; the last payment absorbs the rounding residue; dates are the start
  date plus n months, clamped to month end.
