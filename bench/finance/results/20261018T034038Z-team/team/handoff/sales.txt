**Assumptions (for README, spec left them open)**
- An invoice needs at least one --line (user error otherwise).
- Negative quantity or price is a user error; a zero quantity or price is allowed.
- Line description must be non-empty; line format is exactly 4 fields split on ";" (so ";" cannot appear in descriptions).
- `--status` on invoice list is restricted to draft/issued/partial/paid (argparse choices).
- Payment ACCOUNT must be an asset account and must not be Assets:Receivable itself.
- Payment amount must be positive and at most the balance; rounded half up to cents first.
- Issue numbering counts already-numbered invoices in the same year (per spec), so numbers are not reused.
- Invoice account postings (Assets:Receivable, Income:Sales, Liabilities:SalesTax) are created in the book currency via ensure_account; a pre-existing account in another currency makes issue/pay fail as "does not balance" (post_transaction behaviour).
- Tax rate for `tax add` must be a non-negative number; it is stored and shown exactly as given.
- Customer terms must be a non-negative integer (default 30).
