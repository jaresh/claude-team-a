ASSUMPTIONS
- Invoice number is INV-YYYY-NNNN, with YYYY taken from the invoice date (not the issue date) and
  the counter stored in meta key invseq:YYYY, incremented at issue only.
- Issue posts Assets:Receivable (+total), Income:Sales (-net), Liabilities:SalesTax (-tax, only when
  tax is non-zero). Accounts are created with core.ensure_account.
- Bill posts each line's account (+net), Assets:TaxReceivable (+tax, only when non-zero) and
  Liabilities:Payable (-total). Bill line accounts must be expense or asset type.
- Invoice payment account must be asset. Bill payment account must be asset or liability. Posting:
  invoice payment Assets:Receivable -amount; bill payment Liabilities:Payable +amount.
- Payments must be above zero and no more than the current balance. Dates are YYYY-MM-DD.
- Terms are whole, non-negative days (default 30). Due date = date + terms.
- Tax rate is a non-negative decimal stored as the text given. Line tax = q(net * rate / 100),
  rounded per line. Empty tax code means no tax (stored as null).
- Quantity and price are decimals; quantity is not range-checked (negative quantity is allowed).
- Invoice status: draft -> issued -> partial -> paid. Bill status: open -> partial -> paid.
  Status after a payment is derived from the balance.
- Aging at date D counts only non-draft documents dated on or before D, using payments dated on or
  before D. Days late = D - due. Buckets: current (<= 0), 1-30, 31-60, 61-90, 90+.
- Tax report: collected = tax on non-draft invoices dated in range; paid = tax on bills dated in
  range. by_code lists only non-empty codes.
- Duplicate customer or vendor names are rejected. A bill ref must be unique per vendor.
- Invoice lines are given as DESCRIPTION;QUANTITY;PRICE;TAXCODE (semicolons not allowed in text).
  Bill lines are DESCRIPTION;AMOUNT;ACCOUNT;TAXCODE.
