Assumptions:
- Report currency defaults to the book currency; --currency converts each account's raw sum at the report date.
- Income and liabilities/equity are shown negated (credit-positive); retained earnings = income minus expenses to date.
- Ledger converts each posting's amount into the account's currency at --to, and includes descendant accounts.
- networth loops month by month from --from to --to and uses month_end of each month for the balance sheet.
- Loan: r = rate/1200; payment = q(P·r/(1−(1+r)^−n)), or q(P/n) at 0%; each row's interest = q(balance·r); the last row repays the remaining balance so the closing balance is exactly 0.00.
- All money is Decimal; output via core.fmt.
