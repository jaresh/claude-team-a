Assumptions (for README)
- Line tax = net * rate of tax_code, rounded half up to cents. Lines without tax_code carry no tax.
- Issue posts Assets:Receivable +total, Income:Sales -net, and Liabilities:SalesTax -tax only when tax is nonzero.
- Pay posts [(payment account, +amt), (Assets:Receivable, -amt)]. Amount must be > 0, at most 2 decimals, and at most the balance.
- Payment account must be an existing account in the book currency (system accounts too).
- Price and payment amounts with more than 2 decimals are rejected; quantity may have decimals and is shown as given.
- Due date = invoice date + customer terms (days). Customer terms default 30, must be >= 0.
- Invoice numbers: INV-YYYY-NNNN, sequence per invoice-date year over issued (non-draft) invoices, assigned at issue.
- Drafts have number null and can be created by any editor; issue and pay are subject to closed-period checks via post_transaction.
- Invoice and customer ids: invoices have their own AUTOINCREMENT id from 1; customers are keyed by name.
