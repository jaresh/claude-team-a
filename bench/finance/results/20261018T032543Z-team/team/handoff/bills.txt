Assumptions (for README.md)
- vendor terms default 30 days; due = bill date + terms.
- Bill status: open (no payments), partial, paid. Same status names as invoices.
- bill create: line account must be an existing expense or asset account in the book currency; a negative line amount is rejected; a line with an empty tax code has no tax.
- Tax per line = round half up(net x rate / 100), summed per bill.
- Liabilities:Payable and Assets:TaxReceivable are created in the book currency if missing (only when needed for the posting).
- Duplicate bill ref per vendor is rejected; the same ref may be used by different vendors.
- bill pay: payment account must be an existing asset account in the book currency; amount must be positive and at most the balance; a paid bill cannot be paid again.
- Closed periods are enforced by core.post_transaction for both bill create and bill pay.
- bills_open and bill_tax_by_code consider bills dated on or before `at` (or in [frm, to] for tax); bill_tax_by_code uses the bill date, not the payment date.
