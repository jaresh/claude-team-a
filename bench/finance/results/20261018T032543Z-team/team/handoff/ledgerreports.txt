ASSUMPTIONS (for README)
- Reports convert each account's total at the report date (balance-sheet, trial-balance, networth at the month end; income over the period end date), then round per account, so rows and totals add up.
- Income and liability and equity are shown as negated raw balances (positive = normal balance).
- retained_earnings = income minus expenses to date; total_equity = equity + retained_earnings.
- networth `--from`/`--to` are YYYY-MM months, inclusive; each month is valued at its last day.
- trial-balance and ledger use book currency only; ledger rows are converted to the account's currency at the period end.
- Income statement lists every income/expense account with postings in the period, including zero totals (e.g. Expenses:Gifts reversed to 0.00).

KNOWN LIMITATION
- In multi-currency books, per-account rounding can make total_assets differ from total_liabilities + total_equity by 0.01,
  and trial-balance total_debit and total_credit can differ by 0.01. Single-currency books are exact.
