Assumptions
- Empty line description, negative quantity or price, and a missing tax code on a line that needs one are errors.
- Customer terms are 0..3650 days, default 30. Tax rate must be >= 0.
- Due date = invoice date + customer terms.
- Invoice numbers are INV-YYYY-NNNN, counted per year on issue. Drafts have no number.
- Issue posts Assets:Receivable (total), Income:Sales (net), Liabilities:SalesTax (tax, omitted when zero).
- Pay: amount > 0, must not exceed balance, account must exist, be an asset, and not be Assets:Receivable. Posts account +amount and Assets:Receivable -amount. Writes a payments row; status becomes partial or paid.
- Quantity and price are stored and shown as given text.
- Aging counts invoices with status != draft dated <= --at, using payments dated <= --at. Buckets are days past due: current (<=0), 1-30, 31-60, 61-90, 90+. Payables aging reads bills and bill_lines directly.
- Tax report: collected from issued invoice_lines and paid from bill_lines dated in the period. by_code lists codes used in the period. net = collected - paid. --from > --to is an error.
- Bills in tests are inserted by SQL, so tests do not depend on fin/bills.py.
