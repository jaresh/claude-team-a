Assumptions
- serve listens on 127.0.0.1 only; single-threaded HTTPServer; each request opens the book again.
- POST routes: 401 when X-User is missing or unknown, 403 for viewers, checked before the body is parsed. GET needs no user.
- A GET on /invoices/ID checks that the invoice exists first, so a missing one is 404 rather than the 400 that `invoice show` would raise.
- Invoice line fields containing ';' are rejected with 400 (the CLI splits lines on ';').
- Body values may be strings or numbers; booleans and objects are errors. A missing tax_code means no tax.
- Access logs from BaseHTTPRequestHandler go to stderr (visible in the serve log).
- Non-GET/POST methods get the stdlib's HTML 501 response, not a JSON error.
