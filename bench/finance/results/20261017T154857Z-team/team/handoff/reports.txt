Assumptions to record in README.md:
- Amounts are in the book currency unless --currency CUR is given. Conversion uses Book.to_currency at the report date (--to for income, --at for balance-sheet and trial-balance, each month's last day for networth). Each account is rounded once with fmt, and totals are sums of the rounded account figures.
- Income, liabilities and equity are shown positive. Expenses are shown positive. Assets are shown as they are.
- retained_earnings = all income minus all expenses up to the report date; total_equity includes retained_earnings; total_assets = total_liabilities + total_equity is an identity in book currency.
- --from later than --to is a user error (income, ledger, networth).
- Ledger converts every posting at the --to date. Opening balance covers the account and its descendants before --from. Closing equals the last row balance, or the opening balance when there are no rows.
- Ledger rows are ordered by date, then txn id, then posting position.
- Trial balance lists every account that has postings up to the date, sorted by name. Each row shows the balance on its natural side and "0.00" on the other side.
- Missing FX rates surface as user errors (exit 2) from Book.to_currency.
- Caveat: with --currency, per-account rounding can make balance-sheet or trial-balance totals differ from the cent-exact sum of raw figures. This follows the plan's per-account rounding rule.
- Reports need no --user; they are perm "read".
