Assumptions (in code and tests)
- Tax report: invoices with status <> 'draft' and bills, by document date, both ends inclusive. Only lines with a tax_code count; collected = invoices, paid = bills. Payments do not change tax. by_code lists every code that appears on a line, including zero-rate ones (shown as "0.00").
- Aging: balance = total minus payments dated on or before --at. Invoices dated after --at and drafts are excluded. Fully paid balances (<= 0) are left out. Buckets by days past the due date: current (<=0), 1-30, 31-60, 61-90, 90+. Bills are only in --payables; invoices only in the default receivables mode.
- Reconcile: cleared marks live in the cleared table (account, txn_id) and persist across runs. Only postings to exactly ACCOUNT (not child accounts) dated on or before --date count. Each --clear id must have such a posting, else UserError and nothing is marked (the Book rollback also covers this). Repeated ids are de-duplicated. The audit entry (action 'reconcile', target = account) is written only when --clear marks something. Output: account, date, statement_balance, cleared_balance, difference (statement minus cleared), uncleared (ascending txn ids, not cleared, on or before date).
