- Assumptions implemented (list these in README.md):
  - Tax rates are stored as given text (e.g. "20", "5.5"); tax codes match [A-Za-z0-9_.-]+; rates must not be negative.
  - Customer terms default to 30 days; due = date + terms.
  - Invoice lines are stored as JSON in invoices.lines: description, quantity (text as given), price (2dp), tax_code (or null),
    net and tax (rounded to cents per line). Line net = quantity * unrounded price, rounded once.
  - A line description may contain ';' (the --line arg is split from the right: DESCRIPTION;QUANTITY;PRICE;TAXCODE, empty TAXCODE = no tax).
  - Draft invoices have number NULL and post nothing. Issue assigns INV-YYYY-NNNN (counter per year of the invoice date, from existing numbers) and posts
    Assets:Receivable (total), Income:Sales (-net), and Liabilities:SalesTax (-tax, only when tax != 0). Missing accounts are created in the book currency.
  - A zero-total invoice can be issued but has zero balance, so it cannot be paid.
  - Payment: amount must be positive and at most the balance; account must be an existing asset account and not Assets:Receivable.
    Posts Payment INV-... as a txn; invoice_payments gets (invoice_id, date, amount, txn_id). invoices.paid and status
    (partial/paid) are kept in sync.
  - Closed periods: issue and pay raise UserError via Book.post_txn and change nothing.
  - Roles: write commands need a non-viewer user; reads need no user. Audit actions: tax.add, customer.add, invoice.create, invoice.issue, invoice.pay.
